What is being modeled?

48 massive stations orbit a moving Sun. REBOUND IAS15 computes mutual Newtonian gravity. Each station represents 10¹⁸ kg; total allocation is 0.000008 Earth masses, within the recorded asteroid-belt budget. This is a flat starting cloud, not the full Solar System.

The packet is 10¹² kg. Ideal bidirectional actuators exchange equal and opposite central impulses within 0.65 AU, bounded to 2×10⁹ N and 2×10¹⁴ W per link. These are experimental capability assumptions, not demonstrated hardware. Electrical stores and heat account for 90% regenerative efficiency. There is no free refueling.

A simple budget screen chooses attempt or stand aside. Plans and observations travel at light speed. Local proportional control aims toward circular motion near 1.2 AU; nearest neighbors damp relative motion. No orbit is clamped. “Bound now” is a diagnostic, not proof of durable capture. Stand aside disables coupling; point masses have no collision bodies, so avoidance safety is not modeled.
