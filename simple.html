Physics, assumptions & source

900 resolved masses by default. REBOUND 4.6.0 computes full mutual Newtonian gravity, including the moving Sun. WHFast uses longer coasting steps and finer encounter steps. A numerical energy guard retries smaller steps; IAS15 is the validation reference.

Every central coupling impulse has an equal and opposite counterpart. Electrical stores and retained heat balance exact kinetic work. Force, power, thermal and orbital disturbance limits can veto actions. The local rule predicts from light-delayed packet observations; a simple global budget screen attempts capture, assists, or declines. These are experimental control rules, not optimal coordination.

9×10²⁰ kg is allocated within the provisional asteroid-belt budget. Changing element count shares that fixed allocation. Coupling reach is an idealized 2 AU. Hardware, field propagation, collisions, cooling and relativistic transport are not modeled. Launch speed is limited to 3000 km/s (about 0.01c); higher speeds need a relativistic model. A bound orbit is not proof of stable parking.

Guide markers are not simulated planets. Display radii are enlarged. Trails contain computed history; interpolation only joins already-computed samples. Playback targets can exceed hardware throughput; actual rate is shown.

New scenes are saved locally, without a server session. Older server backups remain untouched.
